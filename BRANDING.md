# Ruffensteint branding

The software license does not grant trademark rights in the Ruffensteint name or logo. This policy does not claim that any mark is registered.

Forks and ports may accurately credit their origin, for example: “Based on Ruffensteint Carving Studio.” Use a distinct product name and icon when distributing a modified application so users do not mistake it for an official Ruffensteint release. Do not imply endorsement without permission.

Keep copyright and license notices required by GPLv3. This branding guidance does not add restrictions to the GPL software permissions.
